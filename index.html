Privacy Policy

1. About this policy

HisabOne ("the App") is a digital khata and ledger manager published by Arduino Metrix ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the App uses, where it is stored, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to this policy. This policy is published in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, as applicable in India.

2. Your data stays on your device

All ledger entries, party and customer names, amounts, notes, settings and backups you create in HisabOne are stored locally on your device (in the app's local storage). We do not run a server for the App, we do not create accounts, and we do not collect, upload, sell or share your ledger data.

Because we do not hold a copy of your data, we cannot recover it if you uninstall the App, clear its data, or lose your device. Please take regular backups (see section 5).

3. Permissions the App may request

  • Microphone – only when you tap the Voice button to speak an entry. Speech is converted to text by the speech-recognition service on your phone (normally Google's service), which may send the audio to that provider over the internet. We do not receive or store your audio. You can deny this permission and keep using the App by typing.
  • Biometric / PIN lock – used only to lock and unlock the App on your device if you turn that feature on. Biometric data is handled by Android and is never accessible to us.
  • Internet – used to load a QR-code library and to open links such as WhatsApp, UPI payment apps, phone or SMS when you choose to share or pay.
  • Storage / Downloads – used only to save files you export (backup, CSV, PDF) to your Downloads folder, and to let you pick a backup file to restore.
  • Vibration and audio settings – used for feedback inside the App.

4. Information we do not collect

  • No name, phone number, email, address or other personal details are sent to us.
  • No contacts, SMS, call logs, photos or precise location are accessed.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs are included, and the App shows no ads.

5. Backups and sharing – under your control

When you export a backup, CSV or PDF, or share a statement or reminder through WhatsApp, SMS, UPI or another app, the content leaves the App only because you chose to send it. Once shared, it is handled by the receiving app under its own privacy policy. Please keep backup files safe, as they contain your ledger data.

6. Third-party services

The App may interact with services provided by others, only when you use the related feature:

  • Android / Google speech recognition (Voice entry)
  • WhatsApp, SMS, phone and UPI apps (sharing and payment links)
  • A content delivery network (cdnjs) for the QR-code library

These services have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for their practices.

7. Data security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot normally read. We recommend enabling your phone's screen lock and the App's lock feature. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete entries inside the App, clear the App's storage, or uninstall it. Uninstalling removes the App's local data. Exported files in your Downloads folder remain until you delete them yourself.

9. Your rights (India)

Under applicable Indian law you may access, correct and erase your personal data and may withdraw consent at any time. Since your data is stored only on your device, you can exercise these rights yourself inside the App (edit or delete entries, clear app data, or uninstall). For any other request or grievance, write to the contact below; we will respond within a reasonable time and in any case within the period prescribed by law.

10. Children's privacy

HisabOne is a business and personal finance tool not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always show the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
